Wang Jinbo 王锦波

Hi! My name is Wang Jinbo (王锦波 in Chinese).

I am a final-year Ph.D. candidate in Computational Mathematics at School of Mathematical Sciences, Peking University, very fortunate to be co-advised by Prof. Weinan E since 2022 and Prof. Lei Wu since 2025.

I have interned and researched at Citadel Securities, Tencent Hunyuan, Meituan LongCat, and IAAR Shanghai (now MemTensor).

My research focuses on two directions:

  • Optimization in LLM training: making post-training and pre-training faster and more efficient.
  • Agents for Insightful Research: building more effective agents that solve practical problems.

I am expected to graduate in 2027 and am on the job market. Feel free to reach out!
